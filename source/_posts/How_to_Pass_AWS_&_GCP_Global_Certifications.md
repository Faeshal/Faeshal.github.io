## Certification Overview 🦅

![](https://i.postimg.cc/Z5P27WMP/aws.png)

Above is a picture of all available AWS certifications. **For AWS Cloud Developer & Solutions Solution Architect - Associate cost $150 USD** with 65 questions, either multiple choice or multiple response & 130 minute exam length. **For AWS Cloud Practitioner cost $100 USD** with 65 questions, either multiple choice or multiple response & 90 minute exam length.

![](https://i.postimg.cc/8cDrbnJm/gcppath.png)
Above is a picture of all available GCP certifications. **For GCP Cloud Engineer cost $125 USD** with 50 multiple choice and multiple select questions & 120 minute exam length.

I did all the exams online, not at the test center. AWS use [**PearsonVue**](https://home.pearsonvue.com/Clients/Amazon-Web-Services.aspx) & Google Cloud use [**Kryterion**](https://webassessor.com/googlecloud). All certifications is valid for 3 years.

## Certifications Tips 🚦

- Prepare **at least 2 weeks**, minimum!. Anything less than that is awful. I don't recommend it, especially for people who have full time jobs & side jobs.
- Strengthen fundamentals & read the exam guide from offical docs. Example: [**SAA-C003**](https://d1.awsstatic.com/training-and-certification/docs-sa-assoc/AWS-Certified-Solutions-Architect-Associate_Exam-Guide.pdf)
- Optional. If you have more time, practice on a real cloud console.
- **Mandatory!** Take practical exam. I buy AWS Practical exam from [**Neal Davis**](https://www.udemy.com/user/63f4a578-c67a-456b-916c-ddadf73e9a26/) & buy GCP one from [**gCloud Learn**](https://www.udemy.com/user/sky-certify/) or if the company wants to pay more to get thousand practical exam [**Exam Topics**](https://unlimited.examtopics.com/) could be an option. In my opinion, high quality knowledge is expensive, so I don't hesitate to buy structured material from experts who are competent in their field. If you want the free one, that's up to you.
- Repeat the practical exam and take a note to collect what material that is difficult & often comes up.
- Deep dive into that material (the most difficult & often comes up). Enrich the material from various sources.
- Lastly, study every day, even a little, never skip studying. This is what I implement.

## Attention for online exams! 🚩

![](https://i.postimg.cc/3R3vXcDC/wired.png)

- Use a **wired** internet connection, **don't use wifi**
- Prepare a conducive room. Quiet & private !
- Do not wear a watch
- Put away cellphones, drinks, food and suspicious equipment, make sure the exam table is clean from anything.
- Make sure the webcam works well and can be rotated to show around the room. The proctor will definitely ask about this. So get ready.
  ![](https://i.postimg.cc/9MPBV9y1/ugreen.jpg)
- For Macbook users, who don't have an ethernet port, please use high quality adapter & high performance LAN cable too. I use [**Ugreen**](https://www.tokopedia.com/ugreenofficialstore) products for both. This 2 items are very critical for supporting connections during online exams, so make sure you choose the best ones!
- Prepare your ID Card / Driving License / Passport for verification process before exam start
- Don't mumble / read the exam questions aloud even in small voice. Proctor will immediately reprimand you if that happens.
